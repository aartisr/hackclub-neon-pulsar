# Neon Pulsar: Zero-G Arena

Neon Pulsar is a browser-based multiplayer zero-gravity disc game created by **[Aarti S Ravikumar](https://ai-aarti.com/)** with the [PCSS II Robotics Team](https://pcssiirobotics.org/). Fly into the white disc, throw it into the opposing team's colored goal, and be the first team to score five points.
