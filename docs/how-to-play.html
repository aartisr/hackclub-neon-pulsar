Player guide

How to play

Start a multiplayer match

  1. One player selects Host Arena and shares the full room code or invite link.
  2. Other players select Join Arena and use that link or code.
  3. Choose Team Cyan or Team Magenta in the lobby. The host selects Start Match.

Score points

Fly into the white disc to pick it up. Throw it into the opposing team's colored goal: Cyan attacks Magenta's goal, and Magenta attacks Cyan's goal. The first team to five points wins. A player must throw the disc to score.

Controls

  • WASD moves; Space rises; Shift descends.
  • E throws the disc while carrying it.
  • T opens chat with everyone; Y opens team-only chat.
  • Esc opens and closes the game menu. Use it to switch teams or choose Home to leave.
  • On touch screens, use the flight stick, altitude buttons, Throw button, and chat button.

Leaving and joining late

Switching teams moves you to your new side's starting end and returns the disc to center if you carried it. A guest who joins during an active match spectates until the next round. If the host leaves, the room closes for everyone. A guest choosing Home leaves only their own session.
